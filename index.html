My Story

KM
📍 Bangalore, India
🦷 Founder — DairyDoc (Dental Clinic EMR)
🧪 Co-Founder — Latte Health (Open Clinical AI)

A dentist friend asked me to help him manage his patients. I sat down to understand the problem — and realised the software just didn't exist for clinics like his.

Most dental clinics in India run on a combination of paper registers, WhatsApp reminders, and Excel sheets — or outdated software that wasn't built for how a clinic actually works. My friend didn't need another generic CRM. He needed something that understood odontograms, lab slips, SAP-style batch jobs, GST billing, and the way a dentist actually moves through a day.

So I built it. DairyDoc is a full-stack, on-premise-first Dental Clinic EMR — 100 screens across 18 phases, covering everything from the interactive SVG tooth chart to GST-compliant invoicing to a patient loyalty programme. Built on Next.js, Node.js, PostgreSQL, Docker, and MinIO, with a clear path to SaaS and ABDM/ABHA integration for India's national health network.

Alongside that, I'm a Co-Founder at Latte Health — building open-weight models and privacy-preserving tools for clinical AI infrastructure. The thread is consistent: healthcare software in emerging markets is still stuck in the last decade, and the gap between what practitioners need and what exists is a product waiting to be built.

// WHAT_IM_BUILDING

DairyDoc

A full-stack EMR built for the 3.5 lakh+ dental clinics in India still running on paper, WhatsApp, and memory.

🦷

The Problem

How it started

A dentist friend asked me to help him build something to manage his patients — appointment books, treatment history, billing. I looked at what existed. Nothing fit how an actual clinic works.

The gap

Most dental software is either built for Western markets (wrong pricing, wrong workflow) or is an ancient desktop app that hasn't been updated since 2012. Indian dental clinics are massively underserved.

🏗️

What's Built

Stack

Next.js · Node.js · PostgreSQL · Docker · MinIO — on-premise first, SaaS path built in from day one.

Scope

~100 screens across 18 phases: interactive SVG odontogram, OPD workflow, treatment planning, GST billing, lab order tracking, periodontal charting, X-ray/DICOM viewer, inventory, patient loyalty programme, and ABDM/ABHA integration for India's national health network.
