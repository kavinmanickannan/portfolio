How it started

A friend needed help. The software didn't exist.

A dentist friend asked me to help him manage his patients. I sat down to understand the problem and realised the software just didn't exist for clinics like his.

Most dental clinics in India run on a combination of paper registers, WhatsApp reminders and Excel sheets, or on outdated software that wasn't built for how a clinic actually works. Software built for Western markets has the wrong pricing and the wrong workflow. My friend didn't need another generic CRM. He needed something that understood odontograms, lab slips, GST billing and the way a dentist actually moves through a day.

So I built it. DairyDoc is now about 100 screens across 18 phases, from the interactive tooth chart to GST-compliant invoicing to a patient loyalty programme, with a clear path to SaaS and ABDM/ABHA integration.

Alongside that, I co-founded Latte Health to build open, privacy-preserving infrastructure for clinical AI. The thread is consistent: the people delivering care deserve tools that fit how they work, and that they actually own.
